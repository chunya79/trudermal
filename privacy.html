Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

About this website

This website provides independent information about Trudermal products for New Zealand visitors. Direct product purchases are completed on a Stripe-hosted checkout for Embrace Health Limited. Stripe processes the contact, delivery and payment information entered at checkout under its own privacy terms.

Information you provide

This static website does not create customer accounts or store payment-card details. If you contact us, we use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary.

Technical information and external links

Hosting and linked services may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and request logs for security and operation. When you follow an external link, the destination's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

Do not send sensitive medical or payment information through a general enquiry. You may ask us to correct or delete personal information we hold, subject to legal retention requirements.
